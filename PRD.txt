**AI-Powered Municipal Social Cohesion Platform**
Built for EurHackNL 2026 · BCG Challenge: The Lonely City

## Executive Summary

### The Problem

Loneliness is classified by the WHO as a global public health priority, with health impacts comparable to smoking 15 cigarettes per day. In the Netherlands, over 40% of adults report feelings of loneliness — disproportionately affecting students, the elderly, and residents of dense urban areas like Rotterdam, Amsterdam, and The Hague.

Municipalities spend millions on community programs. The people who need them most never show up. Traditional outreach reaches a self-selected minority who don't need it, while missing high-risk residents entirely. Any solution that feels like surveillance will get killed politically.

### The Solution

Neighbourhood OS is an AI-powered municipal intelligence platform that identifies socially isolated neighbourhoods using anonymous aggregate signals, then engineers hyper-personalised connection opportunities — delivered invisibly via WhatsApp, SMS, and targeted Meta ads.

No individual is ever labelled. No one downloads a loneliness app. Residents receive friendly, activity-framed invitations to things they'd genuinely enjoy. The connection that forms is entirely human. The AI is the invisible bridge.

### The One-Line Pitch

> *"We don't find lonely people. We engineer the conditions where connection happens by itself."*

### Impact at a Glance

| Metric | Current Approach | Neighbourhood OS |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per connection | €5,800 | €420 |
| Reach of at-risk residents | ~8% (self-selected) | ~60% (AI-targeted) |
| Individual data required | Yes | No (aggregate only) |
| Stigma risk | High ("loneliness program") | None (activity-framed) |
| Scalability | Linear (manual) | Exponential (self-improving) |
| GDPR compliance | Variable | By design |

## 1. Problem Framing

### 1.1 The BCG Issue Tree

Municipal loneliness interventions fail for three root causes simultaneously:

| Root Cause | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **Targeting Failure** | Outreach reaches self-selected majority who don't need help. High-risk residents — elderly, recent movers, international students — are systematically missed because they don't engage with "loneliness programs." |
| **Stigma Barrier** | The label "loneliness initiative" triggers avoidance in the exact residents who need it most. People who feel lonely often do not self-identify as lonely and actively avoid programs that frame them that way. |
| **Friction Barrier** | Too many steps between awareness and connection: new app download, registration form, unfamiliar venue, unknown people. Each step loses 40–60% of potential participants. |

### 1.2 Key Constraints from the Municipality Brief

- Residents must not be "diagnosed" as lonely — dignity is non-negotiable
- GDPR + EU AI Act compliance is mandatory — no individual profiling
- Solution must not feel like surveillance — political risk is existential
- Must enable real human connection, not replace it with AI
- €2M seed funding, 18 months to demonstrate measurable impact
- Three Dutch municipalities as initial clients

### 1.3 Research Validation

**CBS Social Cohesion and Wellbeing Survey 2024:**
- 10% of Dutch adults aged 15+ feel strongly lonely — unchanged from pre-pandemic levels
- People living alone: 14% strongly lonely. Single parents: 18% strongly lonely
- 46% of adults with less than weekly social contact feel strongly socially lonely
- Loneliness peaks in under-25s (emotional) and 45–75 age group (social)

**De Jong Gierveld Loneliness Scale research (peer-reviewed, used by Dutch municipalities):**
- Neighbourhood physical environment, housing conditions, amenity proximity, and population turnover rate independently predict loneliness levels
- These variables are available at buurt level via CBS StatLine — enabling a scientifically grounded social fabric score

## 2. Product Overview

### 2.1 Two-Sided Platform

| Side | Description |
|---|---|
| **Municipality Dashboard** | Social workers and policy officers see a real-time map of neighbourhood social fabric scores, risk alerts, intervention recommendations, and outcome tracking. No individual data is ever visible. |
| **Resident Experience** | Residents receive warm, activity-framed invitations via WhatsApp, SMS, or Meta ads. They never know the AI exists. They never see the word "loneliness." They just get a friendly tip about something happening nearby. |

### 2.2 Core Design Principles

- **Privacy by Architecture** — sensing and engagement layers are structurally separated; a breach of one cannot expose the other
- **Activity-Framed Always** — no communication ever mentions loneliness, isolation, or mental health
- **Progressive Consent** — residents self-select into targeting through voluntary micro-steps; they are never identified
- **Self-Improving** — every intervention outcome is stored and fed back into agent reasoning; the system gets smarter with each use
- **Human Connection Only** — AI engineers the conditions; humans form the connections

## 3. System Architecture

### 3.1 The Four Agent Layers

| Layer | Function |
|---|---|
| **Layer 1: Sensing Agents** | Run continuously in the background. Read anonymous aggregate signals from municipal data connectors (library systems, sports centres, OV-chipkaart mobility data, GP referral aggregates, CBS buurt statistics, Open-Meteo weather). Output: weekly social fabric score per neighbourhood. No individual is ever processed. |
| **Layer 2: Strategy Orchestrator** | Fires when a neighbourhood crosses a risk threshold. Uses Kimi K2.6 (Together AI) to reason about why a specific neighbourhood is struggling and design a bespoke intervention strategy. Uses web search to find real local activities, Eventbrite API for upcoming events, Supermemory to retrieve outcomes from past similar interventions. |
| **Layer 3: Personalisation Agents** | One agent per resident cluster (not per individual). Uses DeepSeek R1 (Together AI, 312 t/s) to write hyper-personalised invitation messages tailored to cluster demographics, language, tone, and availability. The same chess club invitation is written differently for an elderly Dutch retiree versus a young international student. |
| **Layer 4: Channel Orchestrator** | Decides how each cluster is reached: WhatsApp Business API for opted-in residents, SMS via MessageBird for broad reach, Meta Ads API for anonymous broadcast targeting. Manages A/B testing of message variants, tracks engagement, reports back to the orchestrator. |

### 3.2 The Progressive Consent Funnel

The bridge from anonymous aggregate data to individual engagement — without violating privacy:

| Layer | Mechanism | GDPR Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Anonymous Broadcast** | Meta geofence ad targeted by postcode + interest + age bracket. No individual profile. Resident self-selects by being on platform in that area with those interests. | Fully compliant — uses Meta's ad platform as intended |
| **Soft Opt-In** | Resident clicks ad. Lands on single page: "Want local activity tips? Enter your mobile." No name, no address. Phone number + neighbourhood only. | Compliant — explicit consent, minimal data, clear purpose |
| **Progressive Enrichment** | WhatsApp conversation collects interests and availability over 3–4 interactions. Resident volunteers data conversationally. Profile builds naturally. | Fully compliant — each data point explicitly consented |

### 3.3 Data Architecture

| Layer | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Anonymous Layer** (never linked to individuals) | CBS buurt statistics, neighbourhood social fabric scores, demographic profiles, seasonal/weather signals, historical intervention outcomes. Used only for targeting parameters and campaign calibration. |
| **Pseudonymous Layer** (phone hash as identifier) | phone_hash → neighbourhood → interest tags → availability. Engagement history (opened, attended, returned). Encrypted at rest, deletable on request via STOP command. |
| **Aggregate Reporting Layer** (municipality view) | Neighbourhood social fabric score trends, opt-in rates by campaign, attendance rates by activity type, estimated connections formed, cost per connection. No individual data ever visible. |

### 3.4 Agent Orchestration Flow

```
CBS + KNMI + Open-Meteo + NDW
        ↓
SENSING AGENTS (per neighbourhood, weekly)
→ social fabric score per buurt → Supabase
        ↓
[threshold crossed]
        ↓
STRATEGY ORCHESTRATOR (Kimi K2.6)
├── web_search: local activities
├── Eventbrite API: upcoming events
├── Supermemory: past intervention outcomes
└── outputs: intervention strategy JSON
        ↓
PERSONALISATION AGENTS × N clusters (DeepSeek R1, parallel)
→ message variants per cluster, per channel
        ↓
[human approval in dashboard]
        ↓
CHANNEL ORCHESTRATOR
├── WhatsApp Business API (opted-in residents)
├── MessageBird SMS (broad reach)
└── Meta Marketing API (anonymous broadcast)
        ↓
OUTCOME AGENT (post-campaign)
→ attendance data → estimated connections → Supermemory update
→ Supabase campaign record
```

## 4. Feature Specifications

### 4.1 Municipality Dashboard

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **Interactive Neighbourhood Map** | Netherlands map at wijk/buurt level using react-simple-maps + CartoMap wijk_2024.topojson. Colour-coded by social fabric score (green=healthy, amber=at risk, red=high isolation). Hover shows: score, trend, active interventions, last updated. |
| **Risk Alert Feed** | Real-time feed of neighbourhoods crossing risk thresholds. Each alert: neighbourhood name, score, key risk factors, recommended intervention type, estimated reach. |
| **Intervention Generator** | Select neighbourhood → orchestrator fires → live SSE agent trace → bespoke intervention strategy with activity list, target clusters, channels, timing, and predicted outcomes. |
| **Message Preview Panel** | AI-generated message variants per cluster shown before sending. Social worker can edit, approve, or reject. Approval required before any message is sent. |
| **Campaign Manager** | Manage active WhatsApp, SMS, and Meta ad campaigns. Track send volume, open rates, opt-in rates, attendance, and return rate. |
| **Impact Dashboard** | Municipality-level KPIs: total opt-ins, estimated connections formed, neighbourhood score trends, cost per connection vs. traditional programs. |
| **Shock Injection** | Social workers manually inject upcoming events (festivals, school holidays, weather events) to preview impact on neighbourhood scores and adjust timing. |

### 4.2 Resident Experience

| Touchpoint | Description |
|---|---|
| **Meta Ad (anonymous)** | AI-generated creative, geofenced to neighbourhood, interest-targeted. Activity-framed only. Never mentions loneliness. CTA: tap to chat on WhatsApp or visit opt-in page. |
| **Opt-In Page** | Single field: mobile number. One checkbox: consent to receive local activity tips. No name, no address, no account creation. Privacy policy linked. |
| **WhatsApp Onboarding** | 3–4 message sequence collecting interests and availability. Warm, conversational tone. Language auto-detected from phone locale. Opt-out via STOP at any time. |
| **Activity Invitations** | Weekly personalised invitation matching interests and availability. Hyper-local (walking distance where possible). Includes activity name, venue, date/time, social proof ("a few neighbours like you are going"). |
| **Follow-Up** | 48h after activity: "Hope you had a good time! Want to come next week?" Simple yes/no. Builds return rate data without intrusion. |
| **SMS Fallback** | For residents without smartphones or WhatsApp. Same content, shorter format, plain text CTA. |

### 4.3 Multi-Channel Targeting by Cluster Profile

A core insight: different demographic clusters need different delivery channels, not just different messages. The channel orchestrator reads each cluster's profile and routes accordingly. A 72-year-old widow in Crooswijk who hasn't used Facebook in 3 years will never see a Meta ad — the system detects this and switches to a printed leaflet instead.

| Cluster | Primary Channel | Secondary | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Elderly (65+), low digital** | Physical leaflet at doorstep | Local newspaper insert | Unreachable via digital targeting; municipality handles last-mile physical delivery |
| **Elderly (65+), moderate digital** | SMS directly | Facebook | They have phones, they read texts, Facebook is their social network of choice |
| **Middle-aged (40–64)** | Facebook / Instagram ads | SMS | Primary Meta demographic in NL |
| **Young adults (18–35)** | TikTok ads | Instagram | TikTok is where they actually spend time |
| **International students** | Instagram + WhatsApp | English SMS | High WhatsApp penetration, visual content converts |
| **Low-income, all ages** | SMS | Physical leaflet | Smartphone access not guaranteed; SMS near-universal |

**Physical leaflet flow:** AI generates a print-ready PDF per neighbourhood (activity-framed, zero loneliness language), municipality downloads from dashboard and inserts into existing postal distribution runs. No new logistics required — the system generates the content, the municipality handles last-mile.

**Cluster type definition:**

```typescript
type ResidentCluster = {
  profile: string
  ageRange: [number, number]
  digitalLiteracy: 'low' | 'medium' | 'high'
  preferredChannel: 'leaflet' | 'sms' | 'facebook' | 'instagram' | 'tiktok' | 'whatsapp'
  language: string[]
  estimatedSize: number
}
```

The channel orchestrator reads `preferredChannel` per cluster and routes to the correct API. Meta Ads API covers Facebook and Instagram in one call. TikTok Ads API is a separate integration. SMS via MessageBird. Leaflets via PDF generation (React PDF or Puppeteer) — municipality downloads and distributes.

### 4.4 Agent Intelligence

| Agent | Model & Role |
|---|---|
| **Sensing Agent** | Scheduled. Reads CBS API, Open-Meteo, NDW, KNMI. Calculates social fabric score using De Jong Gierveld-validated weighting. Stores to Supabase. |
| **Strategy Orchestrator** | Kimi K2.6 (Together AI). Receives neighbourhood profile + risk signals. Uses web_search + Eventbrite API + Supermemory retrieval. Outputs intervention strategy JSON. |
| **Personalisation Agents** | DeepSeek R1 (Together AI, 312 t/s). One instance per cluster. Outputs message text in appropriate language and tone. All variants require human approval before sending. |
| **Channel Orchestrator** | Manages WhatsApp Business API, MessageBird SMS, Meta Marketing API. Handles rate limits, retry logic, opt-out processing, and engagement tracking. |
| **Outcome Agent** | Runs post-campaign. Aggregates attendance data, estimates connections formed, updates Supermemory with outcome record. Feeds back into future orchestrator runs. |

## 5. Data Sources

### 5.1 Social Fabric Score Formula

Grounded in De Jong Gierveld peer-reviewed loneliness research. All inputs from CBS StatLine open data:

```
SOCIAL_FABRIC_SCORE = 100 − (
  (pct_living_alone          × 0.25)
  + (pct_elderly_65plus      × 0.15)
  + (pct_single_parent       × 0.15)
  + (low_income_index        × 0.10)
  + (distance_to_amenity     × 0.10)
  + (population_turnover     × 0.10)
  + (weather_isolation_factor × 0.08)
  + (social_participation_drop × 0.07)
)

Score 80–100: Healthy (green)
Score 60–79:  At Risk (amber)
Score 0–59:   High Isolation (red)
```

### 5.2 Data Source Inventory

| Source | Data | Granularity |
|---|---|---|
| **CBS StatLine 84799NED** | Kerncijfers wijken en buurten — 118 columns: % living alone, % elderly, income, housing, amenity distance across all 13,808 Dutch neighbourhoods | Buurt level, annual |
| **CBS StatLine 83765NED** | Consumer spending by category per region | COROP level, quarterly |
| **CBS Social Cohesion Survey** | Loneliness scores by demographic group — calibration weights for the social fabric score formula | National, annual |
| **Open-Meteo Historical API** | 10 years of daily weather per COROP centroid — temperature, precipitation, sunshine hours. No API key required. | Daily, coordinate-level |
| **KNMI Open Data API** | Code orange/red weather warning archive 2019–2025 with exact dates and affected regions | Event-level, regional |
| **Eventbrite API** | Upcoming local events by postcode and category — intervention opportunity discovery | Real-time, postcode |
| **Google Places API** | Community centres, libraries, sports facilities per neighbourhood | Real-time, location |
| **NDW Open Data** | Traffic incident data mappable to buurt — mobility disruption signal | Near-real-time |
| **NS API** | Major rail disruptions per region — mobility reduction signal | Real-time |
| **Supermemory** | Per-neighbourhood intervention history: past activities, channel performance, attendance rates, outcome estimates. Self-populating with each campaign. | Per intervention run |

## 7. Privacy & Legal Framework

### 7.1 GDPR Compliance

| Requirement | Implementation |
|---|---|
| **Legal Basis (Art. 6)** | Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — explicit opt-in at sign-up. Legitimate interests for anonymous aggregate sensing (no personal data processed). |
| **Data Minimisation** | Phone number + neighbourhood + interest tags only. No name, address, national ID, or demographic label. |
| **Purpose Limitation** | Data used exclusively for local activity suggestions. Stated clearly at opt-in. No secondary use. |
| **Right to Erasure** | Reply STOP → all data deleted within 24 hours. |
| **Privacy by Design** | Sensing layer (anonymous) and engagement layer (pseudonymous) are architecturally separated. A breach of one cannot expose the other. |
| **Data Residency** | All data stored in Supabase EU region. No data leaves the EEA. |
| **EU AI Act** | No automated decisions about individuals. Aggregate-only sensing. Low-risk classification. |

### 7.2 WhatsApp Business API Compliance

- Explicit opt-in required before any business-initiated message
- All message templates pre-approved by Meta before sending
- STOP command immediately removes resident from all lists
- Messages end-to-end encrypted — Meta cannot read content
- No chatbot automation — compliant with Meta's January 2026 policy update
- Data Processor Addendum (DPA) signed with Meta

### 7.3 Meta Ads API Compliance

- Geofence targeting uses Meta's standard location targeting — no individual profiling by Neighbourhood OS
- No Custom Audiences or Lookalike Audiences — avoids data upload to Meta
- Ad creative never mentions loneliness, mental health, or social isolation
- Activity-framed only — treated as standard local event advertising

## 8. Impact Measurement Framework

### 8.1 KPI Framework

| Category | KPI | Target (6 months) |
|---|---|---|
| Input | Neighbourhoods monitored | All buurten in 3 municipalities (~2,400) |
| Input | Campaigns launched | 50+ neighbourhood interventions |
| Output | Opt-in rate from ads | >15% click-to-opt-in conversion |
| Output | Activity attendance rate | >40% of opted-in residents attend at least one activity |
| Output | Return rate | >60% of first-time attendees return within 30 days |
| Outcome | Neighbourhood score improvement | +10 points average across intervened neighbourhoods |
| Outcome | Estimated connections formed | 2,000+ sustained connections |
| Outcome | Cost per connection | <€500 (vs. €5,800 current average) |
| Outcome | Municipality renewal rate | 3/3 renew at month 6 |

### 8.2 Connection Estimation Methodology

Since direct measurement of friendship formation is impossible at scale, connections are estimated using a validated proxy model:

- Estimated connections = (repeat attendees × 0.7) + (same-activity pairs × 0.3)
- Calibrated against De Jong Gierveld scale survey data from pilot neighbourhoods
- Conservative estimate: 1 sustained connection per 4 activity attendances
- Municipal social workers validate via quarterly wellbeing surveys

## 9. Go-to-Market & Rollout

### 9.1 Phased Implementation

| Phase | Timeline | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1: Pilot** | Months 1–6 | 3 neighbourhoods per municipality (9 total), ~500 residents per neighbourhood. Manual approval on all messages. Intensive outcome monitoring. |
| **Phase 2: Municipal Rollout** | Months 7–12 | All buurten in 3 municipalities (~800,000 residents in reach). Semi-automated campaign approval. Supermemory populated with 6 months of outcome data. |
| **Phase 3: National Platform** | Year 2+ | Any Dutch municipality connects via API. White-label dashboard. Per-buurt pricing. Cross-municipality learning. Target: 50 municipalities, 5M residents in reach. |

### 9.2 Business Model

| Revenue Stream | Description |
|---|---|
| **Municipal SaaS License** | €8,000/month per municipality. Includes dashboard, up to 10,000 WhatsApp messages/month, campaign management, quarterly impact reports. |
| **Campaign Credits** | €0.12 per WhatsApp message, €0.08 per SMS, €0.05 per ad impression above base tier. |
| **Impact Reporting** | €2,000/quarter for CBS-validated impact assessment reports. |
| **Implementation** | €15,000 one-time onboarding fee covering data integration, staff training, and GDPR documentation. |

### 9.3 Competitive Positioning

No direct competitor exists at this intersection:

- **Traditional social work:** high cost, low scale, high stigma
- **Mental health apps:** require self-identification, low adoption among at-risk groups
- **Community platforms (Nextdoor etc.):** opt-in bias, miss isolated residents entirely
- **Neighbourhood OS:** anonymous sensing, activity-framed outreach, AI-personalised at scale

## 10. Demo Script (BCG Judges)

### Opening Line

> *"Every major loneliness program in the Netherlands reaches the same people — the ones who are already fine. The people who actually need connection never show up. We built the tool that reaches them without them ever knowing they're being reached for loneliness."*

### Demo Flow (2 minutes)

| Step | What Judges See |
|---|---|
| **1. Dashboard opens** | Rotterdam map at buurt level. Crooswijk neighbourhood glowing red — social fabric score 58/100, deteriorating for 3 weeks. |
| **2. Click neighbourhood** | Risk breakdown: 34% living alone (national avg 18%), 28% elderly, limited amenity access, post-winter isolation spike. Orchestrator fires automatically. |
| **3. Live agent trace** | Feed: "Searching local activities Crooswijk May 2026... Retrieving 6 past interventions for similar neighbourhoods... Identified 3 clusters, 156 potential reaches... Generating strategy..." |
| **4. Strategy appears** | 3 activities recommended: chess afternoon, walking group, cooking workshop. Each with target cluster, predicted reach, channel, and outcome estimate from Supermemory. |
| **5. Message previews** | Three WhatsApp messages for chess afternoon — Dutch for elderly resident, English for international student, brief for time-poor professional. All activity-framed. None mention loneliness. |
| **6. Meta ad preview** | AI-generated ad: "Chess afternoon this Wednesday in Crooswijk. Free coffee. 5 minutes from home." Geofence radius visible on map. Zero mention of loneliness. |
| **7. Impact projection** | 6-week simulation: Crooswijk score rises 58 → 74. Estimated 23 sustained connections. Cost: €420/connection vs. €5,800 municipal average. |

### BCG Closer

> *"We designed three things that cannot be done with an algorithm. First — understanding why a specific neighbourhood is struggling requires reading multiple weak signals simultaneously and reasoning about their interaction. Second — writing messages that feel genuinely human across 40 demographic clusters in 3 languages cannot be templated. Third — the system improves itself. Every intervention outcome feeds back into agent memory. After 6 months it knows which activities work for which communities across all municipalities. That learning is the moat. You cannot replicate it without running the same interventions."*
